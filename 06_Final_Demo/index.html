Conclusion

Answer to the project question

Yes, AI can write a useful first draft quickly. The parts most likely to be wrong are the financial numbers and the market statistics.

  • AI sounds confident even when it invents figures, so every number must be checked.
  • Use AI for structure and wording. Use people and tools like Sheets for the facts.
  • Responsible use: label assumptions, keep an error log, and never present AI figures as facts.
